# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This is an academic project maintained by faculty and students. Contributors
come from institutions with different accreditation regimes, different levels of
experience, and different first languages. Assume good faith and be precise
rather than clever.

Expected:

- Give and accept correction on technical grounds. A wrong outcome statement is
  a fact to fix, not a matter of opinion or standing.
- Cite sources. "The manual says X on page Y" ends a disagreement; "I'm fairly
  sure" does not.
- Be patient with contributors transcribing frameworks in a language that is not
  their first.

Unacceptable:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Publishing others' private information, including student data
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to
anyone representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer named in `MAINTENANCE.md`, or to the lab head
if the concern involves the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses, in proportion: a private correction, a public warning, a temporary
block, or a permanent ban. The maintainer decides and records the reasoning.
